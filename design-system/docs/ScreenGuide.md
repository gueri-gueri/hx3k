How to get a converted capture onto the HX Stomp: what it needs, four steps from the app, the same by hand with HX Edit, and what to know.

A showcase of the app's own screen, not a component: there is nothing to mount. Source: `src/renderer/src/views/Guide.tsx`.

A `page page-narrow`: the `page-head`; a single-row card (`card setting`) for the prerequisite, with a link to HXtra's setup page; the `Steps` in their large size, the second one carrying a button to the Converted screen; the `Notice` about the .wav; a `section` "With HX Edit instead", with a muted lead and the two steps that replace the first two, the second carrying Open folder and the folder's path; and a `section` "Good to know" with a card of `note` rows.

The steps by hand follow HXtra's own setup page, and name HX Edit's controls as HX Edit writes them. On Windows they are the only way, and the lead says why.

`Shell` in the example is the `ScreenTones` card's wrapper; it is not exported.
