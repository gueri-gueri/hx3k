How to get a converted capture onto the HX Stomp: what it needs, four steps, and what to know.

A showcase of the app's own screen, not a component: there is nothing to mount. Source: `src/renderer/src/views/Guide.tsx`.

A `page page-narrow`: the `page-head`; a single-row card (`card setting`) for the prerequisite, with a link to HXtra's setup page; the `Steps` in their large size, the second one carrying Open folder and the folder's path; the `Notice` about the .wav; and a `section` "Good to know" with a card of `note` rows.

The steps follow HXtra's own setup page, and name HX Edit's controls as HX Edit writes them.

`Shell` in the example is the `ScreenTones` card's wrapper; it is not exported.
